Delete Your Account

You can delete your Curiositi account and all associated data at any time. This action is permanent and cannot be undone.

Option 1 — Delete from the app
1
Open Curiositi on your device
2
Tap the Profile tab at the bottom of the screen
3
Tap Settings
4
Scroll down and tap Delete Account
5
Confirm deletion when prompted. Your account will be permanently deleted within 30 days.
Option 2 — Request deletion by email

If you cannot access the app send us an email from your registered email address. We will delete your account within 7 days.

Email privacy@curiositi.app
What data is deleted and what is kept

When you delete your Curiositi account all personal data is permanently removed within 30 days. This cannot be undone.

Name and email address Deleted
Learning topics and quiz scores Deleted
Saved bookmarks and library Deleted
Synthesis Lab fusions Deleted
Profile, preferences and language settings Deleted
Push notification token Deleted
Device identifier (anonymised) Deleted within 30 days
Safety logs (anonymised cryptographic hash only — no personal data, no query text) Kept 7 years

Safety logs contain only a one-way cryptographic hash. Your identity and the original text of any query cannot be recovered from these logs. They are retained solely for platform safety compliance.
